About the Project

India's Story, Told in Frames

Aravind Adiga's The White Tiger — winner of the 2008 Booker Prize — follows Balram Halwai from the dusty poverty of rural Bihar to a Bangalore office under a chandelier of his own. This project turns that journey into a fully animated 30-minute video summary: every scene has kinetic typography, spring-physics animations, scene-specific cinematic colour palettes, and a narration script timed to the exact frame.

Core Themes

Five Ideas That Run the Book

01
The Rooster Coop
Servants maintain their own captivity out of love for their families. Only a man willing to let his family be destroyed can break free.
02
Light vs. Darkness
India is two nations — the coastal tech cities of Light and the feudal Gangetic plain of Darkness. They coexist without seeing each other.
03
Master-Servant Dynamic
“Do we loathe our masters behind a facade of love, or love them behind a facade of loathing?” Balram lives inside this question for years.
04
Democratic Illusion
Elections are theater. The Great Socialist takes the largest bribes. Real power belongs to the landlords and the red bag.
05
Entrepreneurship as Freedom
White Tiger Drivers. Built on stolen money, corrupt licenses, and the dead master's name. Freedom that costs everything — and is worth it.

How It's Built

The Technical Stack

Remotion 4
React-based video framework. 54,000 frames at 30fps. Every animation is a function of the current frame number.
TypeScript
Strict mode throughout. Scene components, hooks, and the audio pipeline are all fully typed.
TailwindCSS v4
Utility-first CSS for all scene layouts. Dark cinematic palettes: slate-950, amber-400, violet-950.
ElevenLabs TTS
Voice: George (JBFqnCBsd6RMkjVDRZzb). Model: eleven_multilingual_v2. Request stitching for seamless scene boundaries.
fluent-ffmpeg
Merges 18 individual TTS segments into voiceover.mp3. Also layers ambient SFX at −15dB for cinematic depth.
useSyncedTiming()
Custom hook that locks visual animations to real ffprobe-measured audio durations. Zero drift between narration and visuals.
